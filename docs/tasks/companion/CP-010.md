# CP-010 — Review the native companion readiness gate

## Outcome
A recorded review decides whether the real shell-based companion meets its accepted feature scope before publication work begins.

## Baseline and scope
Review CP-001 through CP-009, CX-007 and subsequent scope changes, the current shell qualification and [companion PRD](../../product/COMPANION-PLUGIN-PRD.md). A native boot or a passed concept suite cannot close this gate.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Every accepted feature maps to actual native implementation and evidence; unported behavior and deliberate deferrals remain explicit.
- [ ] The companion genuinely reuses the qualified shell, and independent consumers/exported projects remain functional without it.
- [ ] Persistence, editor interactions, optional preparation, generation, fixtures, design-system export and recovery are qualified within the agreed scope.
- [ ] Required platform, security, accessibility and performance gaps keep the gate blocked; no thresholds or requirements are silently weakened.
- [ ] Exact foundation/concept/native candidate identities and residual supported limits are recorded; later material changes reopen affected qualification.

## Verification
Review evidence against current source and rerun the agreed candidate regression set. Confirm three native product journeys and independent-consumer proof, with failed/skipped/not-run states preserved.

## Evidence and handoff
Not executed. Produce an explicit pass/blocked review with owner/reviewer, artifact hashes, actual results and remaining restrictions. Passing starts the publication lane; it is not permission to tag, publish, submit a listing or change permissions.
