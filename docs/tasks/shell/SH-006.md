# SH-006 — Complete shared feedback and event contracts

## Outcome
Consumers receive consistent errors, progress, notices and localized feedback without duplicating infrastructure.

## Baseline and scope
Reuse [typed events](../../architecture/EVENT-BUS.md), [errors/notifications](../../architecture/ERRORS-AND-NOTIFICATIONS.md), [logging](../../development/LOGGING-AND-DEBUGGING.md) and modal/notice services. Cover operation cancellation, uncertain outcomes and structured diagnostics required by future companion workflows.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Committed facts are emitted only after persistence; observer or notice failures do not relabel a committed write as a failed create.
- [ ] Feature publishers/subscribers remain scoped, with owned disposal and independently observed subscriber errors.
- [ ] Error contracts explain known side effects and safe next actions; raw causes, secrets and note contents do not leak into exports.
- [ ] English/German and pending-locale behavior stay explicit; long text and unavailable translations do not hide actions.

## Verification
Exercise real services with failed persistence, throwing/rejected subscribers, broken notice delivery, cancellation and late completion. Run event/catalog and locale checks, and verify user-visible recovery states in a consumer harness.

## Evidence and handoff
Not executed. Record covered error/event catalogs, redaction tests, actual interaction results and manual accessibility gaps.
