# Issue desk

## Outcome

A service team can capture, browse and resolve requests.

## First iteration

Capture a subject, list requests, and open the details of one request.

## Acceptance

An empty list has a clear empty state. Validation failures do not lose entered data.

This example is source material, not an instruction to execute scripts or fetch remote data.
